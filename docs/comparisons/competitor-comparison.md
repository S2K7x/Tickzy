# Competitor Comparison (positioning, strengths, weaknesses)

- **Last updated:** 2026-11-19 · Sources: competitor dossiers in `docs/research/competitors/`; reviews in `docs/research/customer-feedback/review-analysis.md`.

| Vendor | Positioning | Best at | Weakest at | Price signal | Threat to Tickzy |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Pylon** | Agentic B2B support + CS platform; 1,800+ cos | Slack Connect depth, account intelligence, breadth (13 integration categories), AI lifecycle (backtest, QA) | price (~$100k median contracts), min seats, gating (Teams/portal/RBAC $139), stringly webhooks, low API limits, growing complexity | $89–139/seat + credits | **Highest** (same ICP) |
| **Plain** | API-first agentic support infra for technical B2B | DX (GraphQL parity, typed webhooks, customer cards), rigorous AI safety design, keyboard speed | seat caps (≤5/≤10), fewer channels, less CS depth, search | $45–99/seat + credits | **High** (dev-tools ICP) |
| **ClearFeed** | Slack-native helpdesk (external/internal/bridge) | Slack triage-channel workflow, usage pricing, cheap | polish, reporting depth | $24–49/agent or $40–80 usage | Medium (low end) |
| **Thena** | AI B2B support, "AI is the baseline" | AI included, AI logs, reply-as-yourself | smaller scale, unclear momentum | free/$29–119 | Medium |
| **Foqal** | Slack/Teams ops platform (IT/HR/CS) | ESM workflows, approvals, IdP actions | B2B CS depth | $50–75/operator | Low–Medium (adjacent) |
| **Unthread** | AI-native helpdesk/ITSM in Slack/Teams | ITSM in Slack (CMDB, CAB) | external B2B depth | demo-led | Low–Medium |
| **Intercom/Fin** | Customer Agent company; being acquired by Salesforce | best-in-class AI agent pipeline (custom models), outbound | B2B account model, price predictability, post-acquisition uncertainty | $29–132/seat + $0.99/outcome | Medium (AI bar-setter; migration opportunity) |
| **Zendesk** | Enterprise default "Resolution Platform" | completeness, ecosystem, enterprise trust | complexity, cost stacking, weak Slack Connect | $19–115+/agent + Copilot $50 + resolutions | Medium (displacement target) |
| **Front** | Collaborative inbox for client communication | email collaboration UX, Channel API | Slack-shared-channel support, add-on stacking | $25–105 + AI | Low–Medium |
| **Freshdesk** | Mid-market value helpdesk | price, breadth, day passes | B2B Slack, innovation pace | $19–89 | Low |
| **Help Scout** | Simple human support for SMB | simplicity, free tier, $0.75 AI | B2B account depth (company grouping paid) | free/$25–75 | Low |
| **Gorgias** | Ecommerce conversational AI | commerce actions, volume pricing | not B2B SaaS | per-ticket | None (lessons only) |
| **OSS (Chatwoot, Zammad, Libredesk)** | Self-host omnichannel | control, cost | B2B Slack Connect, AI depth | free/self-host | Low (signal of demand) |
| **AI layers (Sierra, Decagon, Forethought, Parahelp, Inkeep)** | Agents on top of helpdesks | autonomous resolution | not systems of record | enterprise contracts | Partners (connected agents) more than competitors |

## Where Tickzy can win (summary — detail in `docs/tickzy/product-opportunities.md`)
1. Pylon-level B2B depth with **Plain-level developer platform** and **no paywalls on basics**.
2. **Trustworthy AI** (conservative, evaluated, explainable, cheap, BYO).
3. **Email + Slack both first-class**.
4. **Configuration you can test** (simulate routing/SLA/workflows on past data).
5. **Collaborator-friendly pricing** (unlimited free collaborators).
